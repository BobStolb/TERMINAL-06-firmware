## What this board adds and removes against the three it replaces

Compared with the inherited AlexGyver board + TS06-SEC + TS06-COLON, one TS06-MAIN unit needs:

* **new:** the 12 V input stage (jack, PTC, Schottky, 100 µF), an R-78E5.0-1.0 regulator, a TC4420 driver, an LM393 comparator and the rail divider with its trimmer, one anode resistor per digit tube (six), four more TLP627 (six in all instead of the inherited four plus SEC's two), two more backlight LEDs (eight), and in the SMD build a bare DS3231SN with a CR2032 holder;
* **unchanged in kind:** the Nano, the К155ИД1, the two MCP23017 and eighteen MPSA42/MMBTA42 with their base resistors, the two ИНС-1 with their own 220 kΩ ballasts, the eight tubes, the fascia connector;
* **gone:** the inherited board itself, SEC's XS1/XS2/XS3/XS4 and the colon board's connector and harnesses, the 470 kΩ bleed trimmer and the shared 10 kΩ anode resistor of the old converter.
