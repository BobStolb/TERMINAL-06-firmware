## ⚠ CORRECTION — 05.09.26: reading 1 was mislabelled, and it propagated
 
`3d/IN12.FCStd` now carries a parametric model of this tube with a populated spreadsheet.
Cross-checking it against the readings below turns up a labelling error on this sheet that
went on to cause a much larger drawing error downstream.
 
| | This sheet said | `IN12.FCStd` alias | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25.27 mm | "Envelope, **narrow axis** (`env_w`)" | `envD` = **25.50** | **Mislabelled. It is the DEPTH, not a width.** |
| 28.70 mm (mean of 2–3) | "Envelope, wide axis (`env_h`)" | `envH` = **28.86** | Consistent — good |
| — | *never measured* | `envW` = **19.47** | **No caliper support at all** |
| 18.63 mm | Digit numeral height | — | Consistent with an `envH` of 28.86 |
 
**Why it matters:** a sheet that reports a 25.27 "narrow axis" and a 28.70 "wide axis" describes
a tube roughly 25 × 29 in its *face*. The concept plates read it that way and drew the ИН-12А as a
front face about 22 × 24 with **14 mm of depth** — a shallow, wide block. The part is the other
way round: a **19.47 × 28.86 face on a 25.50 mm barrel.** Taller than it is wide, and deep.
 
**Provenance of the model's numbers is itself unconfirmed.** `envW 19.47`, `pinD 0.92`,
`pinFreeL 6.25`, the pin radii and `pipD` may be calipered, datasheet-derived or estimated —
this sheet cannot tell. **Before any of them go on a PCB, confirm which.** `envW` in particular
has no reading on this sheet and is the one dimension a socket footprint cannot be wrong about.

## The architectural consequence — this is the important part
 
**The 12 leads leave the REAR face of the tube.** Not the bottom. That single fact decides how
every ИН-12 product in this project is built:
 
- **The tube cannot stand on a deck over a socket.** There is nothing under it to plug into.
- It **hangs off a board behind it**, facing forward, pins through that board.
- The board is therefore **vertical**, parallel to the tube face, spanning the row.
- Depth stack, front to back: `0` glass face → `25.50` rear face → pins `6.25` into a board.
  With direct-solder into 1.6 mm stock the board's front face sits at **25.50** behind the glass
  and 4.65 mm of pin protrudes at the rear.
- **The whole 25.5 mm barrel is forward of the board** — which serves the Rev E "show more of the
  bulb" goal far better than a deck ever could, because all four flanks are exposed, not just the
  face.
This is confirmed by the prototype itself: the two hand-decorated tube daughterboards each carry a
**ring of pads per tube**, which is exactly what a rear-exit pin field lands in. That observation
was already recorded in `claude/TERMINAL-06-anode-driver-study.md` and was not connected to the
plate geometry at the time.

**`envH` working value: mean of readings 2–3 = 28.70 mm**, against the model's 28.86. Per the
min/mean/max rule this still needs two more samples before it is load-bearing.
 
### The one that was already flagged
 
The concept plates assumed an **18 mm digit** when setting the old brow geometry. Reading 4 —
**18.63 mm** — is that feature measured rather than assumed. The brow is deleted in Rev E, so the
0.63 mm delta no longer threatens anything, but the digit height is now doing new work: it sets
how much of the 28.86 face is lit, and therefore how the row reads at a distance.
 
## Still needed to close out this part
 
- **`envW` on a real tube.** The model says 19.47; nothing on this sheet supports it. Two samples.
- **Confirm the pin field pattern against a tube**, not just against the model — which of the 12
  positions carry which cathode is `secCathodeMap`'s job, but the *geometry* needs a caliper.
- **Socket, separately** — or the decision to skip sockets and direct-solder. That decision now
  sets the board-to-glass distance directly, so it is no longer a detail: it is the depth of the
  whole product. See `TS06-LIB-SOCKET` in the library guide.
- **Tip-off pip location** — `pipD 3.7` is in the model but its *position* on the rear face is
  not recorded here, and it decides whether the tube can sit flush against a board.
- **Getter flash location** relative to the visible face.
- **Depth, front glass to digit plane** — parallax under the rake, now more significant given the
  barrel is 25.5 deep rather than the 14 the plates assumed.
Two more full samples minimum before any of the above go in as `dims.*` aliases — one tube's
numbers are a hypothesis, not a spec.
