# TERMINAL-06 — the ladder
 
**Created 06.09.26**, after a week trapped on P2. This doc exists to stop that happening again.
 
## The two rules
 
**1. One rung per sitting. Finish it, stop, and don't chain into the next one.**
 
**2. If a rung takes more than one evening, STOP and say so.** That is the tripwire. P2 ate a
week because nobody called it after night two. Anything that overruns gets re-scoped or parked
— it does not get pushed through.
 
**Rungs 3–7 cannot reopen the ghosting question.** None of them touch the digit multiplex.
That is deliberate: there is a run of work here with no route back into the swamp.
 
---
 
## Phase 1 — bank what already works (one evening, ~30 minutes total)
 
**Rung 1 — Commit and push the firmware.** *(10 min)*
Right now `BOARD_TYPE 0`, the timing constants, the `MUX_SLOTS` diagnostic and its comment block
all sit on unpushed local branches. That is the best work of the week and it exists in one place
on one machine.
**Done when:** it's on GitHub.
 
**Rung 2 — Set the time and leave it running.** *(5 min)*
Put it where you'll see it. Let it run overnight.
**Done when:** it's still showing the right time in the morning — which also silently confirms
the DS3231, the boost stage and thermal behaviour, three things never yet checked over hours.
 
**Rung 3 — Measure the HV rail.** *(10 min)*
One number, meter on 600 V DC, black lead clipped to ground before power-on. Closes **P3**,
open since the beginning.
**Done when:** a voltage is written into the prototype plan.
 
---
 
## Phase 2 — make it look like a product (one evening each, any order)
 
**Rung 4 — Fit the backlight LEDs.** Six amber on D11 PWM, pads already on the board.
**Done when:** the tubes glow from behind. Biggest visual change for the least work on this list.
 
**Rung 5 — Fit the colon.** 2× ИНС-1, **220 kΩ ballast each, never shared** (spec §3), low-side
MPSA42 on D10.
**Done when:** the colon blinks. This is the rung where it stops reading as a test rig and starts
reading as a clock.
 
**Rung 6 — Caliper the socket.** Seat height above PCB, tip-off pip projection, front-glass-to-
digit depth. Twenty minutes with the calipers.
**Done when:** `TS06-LIB-SOCKET` exists. Unblocks the case geometry, which is currently resting
on assumptions (the 32 mm floor / 17 mm centreline are still guesses).
 
**Rung 7 — Print the chassis and brow, test-fit.** Unattended machine time.
**Done when:** you're holding it and can see whether the brow reveal is right (spec §6).
 
---
 
## Phase 3 — the two that finish it
 
**Rung 8 — Build the SEC module.** 2× ИН-17 + their 2 TLP627s on D2/D13. Then set
`MUX_SLOTS 6` and look.
**This is the payoff rung.** If the empty-slot theory is right, the ghosting simply is not there
— the slots have real tubes and real anodes now. And if it is clean, **re-run the refresh sweep
before accepting the 50 Hz/camera-flicker concession**; that trade was priced under a wrong
model and may not need paying.
*By this point seven rungs are banked, so this one can fail without the project feeling stuck.*
 
**Rung 9 — Rotary fascia artwork.** Pure design, no electronics, no soldering. All the
dial-critical numbers are already measured (26.94 mm body, 6 mm shaft, 6 positions over ~160°).
Good rung for an evening with no patience for hardware.
 
---
 
## Parked, deliberately
 
- **TS06-TUBE** — decide it on tube-reveal geometry after rung 7, not on bleed resistors. Its
  resistor justification is withdrawn.
- **Anode-driver hardware** (MPSA92, HV shift registers) — not needed for this product. Kept in
  the study for QUADRANT and the decatron build.
- **The four fitted ИН-12 bleed resistors** — leave them in. Harmless, and desoldering risks
  joints for nothing.
