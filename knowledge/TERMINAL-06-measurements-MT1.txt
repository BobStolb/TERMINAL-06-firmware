Fourth entry in the Section 3 capture-sheet series (`TERMINAL-06-cad-component-library.md`),
same format as the IN-12, IN-17, and ИНС-1 sheets.

## Reading quality this round
 
Six caliper photos came in; four were shot right-side-up and read with confidence, two
were upside-down and are being handled the same way the IN-17/ИНС-1 sheets handled that
problem — reported as low-confidence with a re-shoot recommended, not guessed into a
`dims.*` value. **Update, 27.08.26:** a seventh photo closed out row 7 — jaws on the
threaded stem itself, past the mounting nut, real caliper reading.

## Worth flagging
 
**Rows 5 and 6 are genuinely two different features, not one number read twice.** Row 5's
jaw span covers roughly the nut-and-washer stack ahead of the bracket; row 6 is a much
tighter closure, more consistent with a single small part — possibly the lever's pivot
boss. Re-shoot both right-side-up before trying to identify them further; guessing which
is which from an inverted display risks mislabelling two real dimensions as one bad one.
 
**Row 12 — lever throw angle — is the one this capture sheet exists to get right for a
parametric keepout model.** For that goal it's still a completely open number. For the
prototype specifically, `claude/TERMINAL-06-prototype-plan.md` has opted to skip deriving
this on paper and instead test-fit the actual lever against a printed brow — worth noting
here so the two documents don't read as contradicting each other on how urgent this row is.
