SCALER-06New concept · plates Rev 202.09.2026
Fourth product · decatron · not a clock · five things to count

Scalernew

A decatron does exactly one thing: it counts pulses too fast for a person to follow, and shows the count as a glow walking a ring of gas. It was built for nuclear instrumentation — the stage between a Geiger tube and a mechanical register was literally called a scaler. Every decatron clock ever built asks the tube to pretend it is counting seconds. Give it something real to count and it stops pretending. Five things, on a six-position switch, so the object still performs in a room with nothing radioactive in it.

PLATE 01

One step per event

Live · five sources

The ring steps once per event, whatever the event is. Switch modes and the whole character of the object changes — Poisson lurching at background, a hard 5 Hz beat off the mains, a scatter that follows the room's noise. Nothing about that motion is a firmware animation, and that is the whole product. Try the mode switch before reading anything else on this page.

Bring near

The СБМ-20 counting whatever is in the room — about 22 CPM of cosmic muons and daughter products. Arrivals are Poisson, so the glow lurches and pauses and never finds a rhythm. This is the mode that cannot be faked, and the mode most owners will only ever see at background.

What you are looking at Ten main cathodes around the ring, with the two rings of smaller guide cathodes between them that actually do the walking — a guide pulse strikes, the glow hops to it, the next guide takes it on, and it lands on the next main cathode. The shift register is made of gas. That is why a decatron needs so few drivers, and why the movement looks the way it does: the glow does not switch, it transfers.

PLATE 02

Five things to count

The correction that saved it

The first draft of this concept had one input: a Geiger tube. It is the most beautiful mode and it has an obvious flaw — almost nobody owns anything radioactive, so on a normal desk the ring steps about once every three seconds forever and the customer never sees the object do the thing they bought it for. A showpiece nobody can demonstrate is a bad product no matter how good the story is.

The fix is not to abandon radiation. It is to notice that a decatron does not care what the pulses are — it is a counter, and anything that closes a contact is a pulse. Five sources on the shop's existing six-position rotary, four of which work in any room on earth.

PositionSourceAdded partsWhat it feels like
1 · BACKGROUNDСБМ-20 Geiger tube, 400 Vtube ≈900 ₽ Poisson lurching, ~22 CPM. The honest one. Accelerates to a smear near a source.
2 · ENTROPYReverse-biased transistor in avalanche breakdown, comparator-gated≈20 ₽ Genuine hardware randomness, in any room, with nothing radioactive on the desk. The same un-fakeable property for a fiftieth of the cost. Digits show a fresh four-figure draw twice a second.
3 · MAINSOptocoupler on the transformer secondary, ÷10 into the ring≈30 ₽ A hard 5 Hz beat. Digits show cycles counted in the last minute — 3000 at exactly 50 Hz, breathing a few counts either side all day as the grid balances. A frequency meter you watch rather than read.
4 · AUDIOElectret capsule + comparator, threshold on the panel≈50 ₽ The ring dances to the room. Least scientific, and the one that sells the object to somebody standing in front of it.
5 · EXTERNALTwo banana jacks≈100 ₽ Bring your own pulses — a photodiode, a reed switch on a bike wheel, a second tube. This is the position that makes it an instrument rather than a toy.
6 · OFF——HV down, ring dark. A 400 V object should have an off position on the front panel.
What this costs: about 200 ₽ and one switch the shop already owns Four extra sources for roughly two hundred roubles of small-signal parts, on a six-position rotary from the same Vladi59 stock already bought for TERMINAL-06 — eleven switches secured against a need for twenty, and this product wants the same A6 resistor-ladder reading the same way. The control scheme, the ladder maths and the debounce discipline all port straight across. The cheapest thing on this page is the thing that makes it demonstrable.
And it makes the radiation mode better, not worse A buyer who can already see the ring dance to their voice understands what they are looking at before they ever meet the Geiger tube. Mode 1 stops being the feature that has to carry the product and becomes the one that explains it — the moment where the same mechanism is driven by something they cannot see, hear or touch. That is a better story than leading with it.

PLATE 03

Which decatron, and why two kinds

Count the pins first

The register already carries a family guide for this, and it decides the design. Sort by base pin count before anything else: 8 pins is a scaler, 13 pins is a commutator, 15 pins is a Swiss import that fits neither Soviet socket. The two Soviet groups do different jobs, and SCALER-06 wants one of each.

GroupBaseCathodes brought outWhat it can do
ОГ-3 · ОГ-4/5/7/8/98-pin РШ 5-1 Two — a zero cathode and the main, plus two guide sub-cathodes You cannot read the position electrically. You watch it. This is the visible ring — and it is the deepest, cheapest decatron on the board: 180 ₽ × 53 at Avito Seller A, 40% under Begrant and three times the depth.
А-101 · А-102/103/106–11013-pin РШ-30 All ten, individually wired to pins Usable as a direct readout, not just a scaler. 190 ₽ × 50 at the same seller — this is the decade the MCU can actually interrogate, and it is why the instrument can display a number at all without an optical hack.
EZ10A / EZ10B15-pin ZB13 — Elesta, Swiss/German. ~21 mm envelope against ~33 mm Soviet, purple hydrogen glow, Latin printing. Do not force it into a Soviet socket. 1 000 ₽ at Begrant — a curiosity, not a supply.
МТХ-90thyratron — Glow-transfer thyratron at 45 ₽ — the classic ring-counter element, better documented and a tenth the price of ТХ19А-3. Build the discrete ring out of these if the decatron drive turns out to be a fight.
Two things about ОГ-3 that look like faults and are not It glows violet, not orange — argon fill, and it is the reason this product's palette is violet rather than the shop's usual amber. And there is an intentional dark coating inside the top of the envelope from manufacture. Neither is damage, and neither is grounds for rejecting a lot. Within a pin-count group the envelopes are otherwise identical, so the printed type number and the glow colour are the only ways to tell ОГ-3 from ОГ-4 or ОГ-9 — read the glass, not the listing.

PLATE 04

One rail, two tubes

The coincidence that makes it cheap

An СБМ-20 Geiger–Müller tube has a plateau roughly 350–475 V and is normally run at 400 V through a several-megohm anode resistor. A Soviet decatron of the ОГ class wants an anode supply in the same 400–475 V band. That is not luck — these parts were designed to sit on the same chassis. One high-voltage supply feeds the whole instrument, which is the single biggest reason this is a buildable product rather than a two-converter science project.

The ring

What it shows

ОГ-3 steps once per detected particle and divides by ten. At background that is a step every two or three seconds — slow enough that you see each individual atom decay announce itself. Near a source it blurs. The visible decade is deliberately un-instrumented: only its zero output goes back to the MCU, as a carry.

The number

What it says

Four ИН-2 show a 60-second rolling count rate, with an ИН-15А beside them lighting the prefix — the symbol tube doing the job it was built for, the same argument that carries METRIC. A clock that displays “k” is decoration. A counter that displays “k” is correct.

The limit

Where it stops

The ОГ class tops out somewhere in the low kilohertz. Background is under 1 Hz, so this never matters in a room — but near a real source the ring will outrun the tube. The firmware must divide before the ring, and say so, rather than letting it stall and look broken.

The framing decides the price, not the BOM A commodity Geiger counter is $100 on any marketplace. If a buyer arrives at this listing thinking “radiation detector”, the listing has already failed and no amount of glass justifies 24 000 ₽. It has to arrive as what it is: a working piece of 1960s counting instrumentation, in which the display is the counting mechanism, that happens to respond to the room. Sell the ring, not the reading. Every photograph should be of the glow, and the video is thirty seconds of the ring at background — pause, lurch, pause — with a source brought slowly into frame.

Shared with the shop

What carries over

The low-side MPSA42 switching, the neon ballast discipline (one resistor per lamp, never shared), the Nano-class MCU, the printed enclosure process and the QC habit of logging every unit's rail voltage. What does not carry: the six-tube multiplex, the rotary ladder, and the 401 Hz ISR. None of P2's ghosting problem exists in this product — there is no multiplex to ghost.

Why this one is worth building while TERMINAL-06 is blocked Week 0 is stalled at P2 on an anode-quench problem that belongs to the six-tube multiplex. SCALER-06 has four statically-driven digits and two gas-discharge counters — it shares the shop's HV discipline without inheriting its hardest unsolved problem. It is the right thing to have on the bench while the ИН-12 driver question is waiting on parts.

PLATE 07

What it must never claim, and what to answer first

Open
This is not a safety device, and the listing must say so It counts ionising events. It is not a calibrated dosimeter, it is not certified for anything, and nobody should make a decision about their safety using it. Every claim in the listing has to be the kind that can be demonstrated on a bench: “steps once per detected particle”, “shows a sixty-second count rate”. No µSv/h figure unless the instrument has been checked against a real reference — and even then, a number that looks like a dose reading invites a use this object must not be put to. Write the disclaimer before writing the listing, not after.
  1. Source an СБМ-20 and price it. The only line in the BOM with no register entry behind it. While there, ask about СТС-5 and СИ-8Б as alternates — different sensitivities, same rail.
  2. Pull the real ОГ-3 passport figures — anode supply, guide-pulse amplitude and width, maximum stepping rate, whether it is a single- or double-pulse type. Forum numbers are not a datasheet, and this is the same rule that caught the ИН-17 dimension error.
  3. Buy four ОГ-3 and step one by hand before designing the guide driver. 180 ₽ each makes this the cheapest de-risking on the whole board.
  4. Confirm the А-101 is worth the extra socket. If the readable decade adds nothing a four-digit nixie readout does not already do, drop it and run two ОГ-3 — the second ring is more beautiful than a number.
  5. Four socket patterns, none priced — РШ 5-1 (8-pin), РШ-30 (13-pin), РШ27 (ИН-2), ПЛ31 (ИН-15А). Ask SLON999, who stocks the things nobody else does.
  6. Verify Avito Seller A at last. Two products now depend on his stock — this ring and the “предок” ИН-9 — and his rating, real name and whether he ships have never been confirmed.
  7. Check the precedent honestly. Decatron Geiger counters exist as one-off hobby builds and write-ups; what does not exist is one sold as a finished object. Read two of those builds before designing the driver — someone has already made the mistakes.
