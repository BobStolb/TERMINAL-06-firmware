## The one thing to resolve before anything else here matters
 
**Which body diameter — 25 mm or 19 mm — is the actual part on order?** Everything else
in this sheet is downstream of that. If it's not obvious from the order confirmation or
listing title, it's worth a direct check before this switch's hole gets laid out
alongside the МТ1 and КМД1-1 holes on the same fascia — a 6 mm difference in body
diameter is not a rounding error at that scale.
