## Worth flagging
 
**Row 3 moved from pure assumption to eye-confirmed, 27.08.26 — still worth a real caliper
pass before it's load-bearing for a batch order.** МТ1's bushing is now a measured 7.85 mm
(this sheet's row 3 was previously "an assumption stacked on an estimate" against МТ1's old
~8–9 mm visual guess). A direct look at both stems side by side found them close enough by
eye to treat as the same hardware. That's good enough to size one prototype fascia hole —
it is not the same as an independent caliper reading on this specific button, and if this
part is ever sourced from a different batch or lot for the ten-unit run, it's worth actually
measuring rather than continuing to inherit МТ1's number.
 
**Rows 1–2 need endpoint identification**, same issue as the МТ1 sheet had. Without
knowing exactly what each caliper span started and ended on, "13.75 mm" and "16.91 mm"
are real numbers attached to an uncertain label.
