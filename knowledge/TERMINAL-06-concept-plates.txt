### ⚠ The ИН-17 moved the face twice before it settled
 
`TERMINAL-06-measurements-IN17.md` Rev 5 has the full account. Short version: the ИН-17 was
assumed at 15 wide, then drawn at **17.58 × 19.20 portrait** from two caliper readings, and both
were wrong. **The tube is not flat.** It is a round Ø20 stem that flattens to a **14 mm face**.
The two readings were two chords of that curve, not a width and a height.
 
Consequences, all of them in the Rev F drawings:
 
- Face **14 × 20**, digit **9.00** against ИН-12's 18.63 — the seconds read at **48%** the height
  of the hours, on half the glass area. Stated on plate 03 as a design consequence with two ways
  out, not left as an accident.
- **The seconds pair is spaced off the stems.** A 3 mm face gap puts the two Ø20 stems 17 apart
  and they touch. Pair centres are **20.5**, which reads at the front as a 6.5 mm gap. Plate 04
  exists for this one number.
- Overall face **204.3** — not the 195 quoted below, which assumed a 15 mm ИН-17.

## Rev F — the architecture, settled 05.09.26
 
The Rev E/F body was **104 mm deep to carry a display stack 27 mm deep**. Three quarters of
the side view was empty case. That, not the tube error, is why the proportion failed — and
the tube error is what made it visible, because a 25.5 mm barrel on a vertical board makes
the real depth budget obvious.
 
**Decision: cheeks and boards. No moulded body.**
 
| | Rev E.2 | **Rev F** |
|---|---|---|
| Face width | 237 | **204.3** |
| Height | 96 | **62** |
| Depth | 104 | **44** |
| Body | raked slab + plinth | **none — two end cheeks, 6 mm** |
| Fascia | printed panel | **a PCB — black mask, legend in silkscreen** |
| Tube mounting | deck + socket underneath | **vertical tube board, direct-solder, rear pins** |
 
### Why the face drops 33 mm
 
The row was laid out for a 22 mm tube. The part is **19.47**. With the gaps rebuilt around the
real widths the row lands at **204.3 including 8 mm margins** — 237 → 204.3. *This is a real
spec change* — it moves the fascia order, the enclosure and every listing photo.
 
**The 195 figure quoted on 05.09.26 was drawn on a 15 mm ИН-17 and is superseded.** The gaps, as
built: 3 inside a pair, 8 at the colon, 8 before the seconds, **6.5 inside the seconds pair**,
12 before the annex, 8 margins. Only the 6.5 is set by something invisible from the front.

### ⚠ The open frame costs a rear closure, and a bottom one
 
**Tube pins protrude 4.65 mm behind the tube board and sit at 185 V.** In Rev E the deck was
the barrier; there is no deck now and no body either, so **the rear board is the only thing
between those pins and a finger.** The main board needs the same treatment underneath. This is
a per-unit QC check, not a design assumption — the same rule Rev E already carried, moved to a
new surface.
 
### Retention — direct-solder for this run
 
**Ten boards already in hand are drilled for tube pins and their holes are too small to take
sockets.** So this run is direct-solder: the tube board's front face lands at exactly 25.5 mm
behind the glass and no seat height enters the depth stack. **Sockets are wanted on future
boards** — replaceability matters for a product and for binning matched sets — and the next
tube-board revision should be drilled for them. *A socket spec was mentioned but did not
arrive; the seat height it would add to the 44 mm depth is still uncharacterised.*
 
### Iteration risk, named
 
The fascia being a PCB freezes the legend at fab time, and **the legend wording is still design
intent** — RUN / TIME / DISP / AMB / INFO / DATE was never decided, and the A6 ladder that
decodes it is unwritten. **Build the fascia as a printed insert first and swap it for the black
board once the legend is frozen.** The cheeks-and-boards structure does not care what the
fascia is made of, so this costs nothing and removes the only expensive mistake available here.
 
### Still unmodelled — updated 09.09.26
 
**ИН-17 and ИН-15 have no FreeCAD model.** Their status has improved but is not closed:
 
- **ИН-15 = ИН-12 in form**, stated 05.09.26, and drawn that way throughout. Only the glyph set
  differs. No separate model needed.
- **ИН-17** is now drawn from a factory outline drawing rather than a guess: 14 × 20 × 22,
  Ø20 stem, 12 leads on a circle, 35 mm free, key = cut-short lead. **The lead pitch and circle
  diameter are still not dimensioned anywhere**, so the footprint cannot be drawn. That is the
  one remaining blocker on this tube and it needs the bench, not the web —
  see `TERMINAL-06-measurements-IN17.md` Rev 5.

## The case change
 
### The diagnosis
 
Rev C's failure was not depth — it was **framing**. A 166 × 21 mm trench turns each ИН-12А into a lit
rectangle in a slot: you see a window, never a tube — no envelope, no flanks, no domed top, no pins,
and the unlit cathode stack cropped by the trench lip. The 12 mm brow overhung it, so at any angle but
dead-on the brow's shadow took the top third of every envelope. **The three-quarter hero is exactly
the shot that geometry fails.**
 
### The move
 
**The chassis steps back under the tubes instead of leaning over them.**
 
- **No trench. No brow.** Both deleted. The safety-orange brow is gone with them — that accent now has
  no home on the object and needs one somewhere else.
- A **plinth rises out of the base slab, inset from each side and set back from the front edge.** The
  tube board mounts on top. Nothing in the chassis is ever in front of, above or beside a tube — the
  deck is a shelf, not a slot.
- Tubes stand fully proud: ~23 of the ИН-12А's 24 mm envelope above the deck line.
- **Backlight moves** into the deck surface behind each socket, grazing the glass from below.
### Geometry (Rev E.1 — corrected, unchanged in E.2)
 
| | Rev E claimed | **Rev E.1, from the drawing** |
|---|---|---|
| Base slab | 237 × 104 × 40 | **237 W × 104 D × 56 H** |
| Plinth | 190 × 46 × 18, **inset 24** each side | **213 W × 46 D × 16 H, inset 12** each side |
| Setback from front edge | 30 | 30 — unchanged |
| Deck height | 58 | **72** (56 + 16) |
| Overall with glass | — | **96** |
| Front rake | 12° | 12° — unchanged |
 
### What drawing it caught
 
Two numbers were wrong in Rev E and neither was arguable once the plan view existed:
 
1. **The plinth was too narrow for its own tube row.** The tube row spans x = 13.5 → 223.5 (the ИН-15А
   annex reaches 223.5). A 190 mm plinth inset 24 each side runs 23.5 → 213.5, so **the last tube
   overhung the plinth by 10 mm and the first sat 10 mm inboard of nothing.** Corrected to 213 wide,
   inset 12. **The "24 mm inset" figure was invented before the annex was drawn and never re-checked
   against it.**
2. **The base was too short to carry its own fascia.** TS06-FASCIA is 52 mm tall and sits on a face
   raked 12°; a 40 mm slab cannot hold it. Corrected to 56 mm, which puts the deck at 72 and the
   overall height with glass at 96 — the same height as the Rev C wedge, which is reassuring rather
   than surprising.
Both are the same class of error: **a dimension asserted in prose, never drawn.** The plan view took
about a minute to produce the contradiction. Plate 01B now carries that plan as a standing check.
 
### Safety consequence — not optional
 
With the trench gone, **the deck's top surface is the only barrier between a customer's fingers and
185 V.** Every pin, socket shoulder and cathode lead must sit strictly below the deck plane, and the
plinth top must be a continuous printed surface with no cable slot open to the top face. **Per-unit QC
check, not a design assumption.**
 
### Dust
 
An exposed tube collects dust in a way a trenched one does not. Real, and worth naming in the listing.
Hood is the mitigation; a soft brush in the box is the cheaper one.
 
### Provisional
 
**Plinth height, setback and rake angle are derived, not measured on a printed part.** One test print
and a sight check before the chassis is committed. The whole revision is a viewing-angle claim, and
viewing angles are checked with an eye.

## What is interpretation, not spec
 
- Glow, glass tint and the faint unlit cathode stack are an interpretation of how ИН-12s look in a dim
  room. Envelope tint varies between batches — hence the one-seller rule.
- **Since Rev E.2 the axonometric views draw the hand-authored cathode paths too**, not type — the
  same wire-stroke glyphs the 1:1 elevation uses, mapped onto the projected face. Effect **timings**
  are the firmware's; brightness curves and light falloff are not, and nothing here is ray-traced.
- The night sheets are a *reading* of a dark room. Nothing computed the falloff; it is hand-authored
  per surface.
- Surface finish reads smoother than 0.20 mm PETG will photograph.
- The heroes are axonometric with no perspective — a real lens will foreshorten the depth.
- The exploded view draws the board as **one plate**. In the real prototype the ИН-12s sit on two
  hand-decorated daughterboards above the main board; the production intent is a single fabricated
  tube board (TS06-TUBE, see `claude/TERMINAL-06-anode-driver-study.md`). Component blocks are
  indicative positions, not a layout.
- The scale sheet's phone (146 × 71) and mug (⌀80 × 95) are nominal typical objects, not a specific
  product.

## Source
 
The drawings are generated by a small dependency-free SVG toolkit kept at `claude/render/` and
mirrored into the `TERMINAL-06-firmware` repo under `render/`. Five files:
 
| File | Produces |
|---|---|
| `tubes.py` | shared primitives — wire-cathode glyph paths, glow filter stack, tube face, knob/lever/button |
| `render.py` | daylight axonometric heroes + dimensioned GA sheets (8 SVGs) |
| `render2.py` | lit 1:1 front elevations, rear panel, tube macro (6 SVGs) |
| `render3.py` | night heroes + night rear three-quarter (5 SVGs) |
| `render4.py` | TERMINAL-06 extended set — left 3/4, profile, plan, exploded, fascia ×2.6, scale (6 SVGs) |
 
Everything is driven from the millimetre constants above. `render/README.md` carries the usage notes
and three traps worth knowing before extending it:
 
1. **The world is y-up; every tube fragment is y-down.** Feed the face matrix a `+y` vector and every
   glyph renders vertically mirrored — and it is hard to spot, because a flipped 3 still looks like a
   3. Check a **4** or a **9**.
2. **`glyph_path()` falls back to the `"8"` path for any character not in the table, silently.**
   Cyrillic **Р** and **П** are now their own entries (U+0420 / U+041F, *not* the Latin lookalikes);
   before they were added the AM/PM tube rendered a perfectly plausible 8 for weeks.
3. **Do not use the axonometric camera for a plan or a profile.** Its depth term grows with height, so
   at a steep angle upper geometry sorts behind lower geometry and the drawing inverts. Elevations are
   built as true orthographic SVG — which is also what makes them measurable. And in those hand-built
   sheets the coordinates are screen-space, **y down**: angle maths moved across from the world-space
   code needs its `cos` negated, or the dial legend fans below the knob instead of around it.
**The older `terminal06-render-source.zip` toolchain is fully superseded.** Its `front.py` still
describes the Rev C trench geometry; do not feed it to anything.
