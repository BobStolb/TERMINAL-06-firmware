# ANIMATIONS — what the glass does, from the source (Rev 3)
 
Every effect the shop's firmware can run on a gas-discharge display, with the real constants read
out of the synced AlexGyver NixieClock v2.5 source rather than estimated. Companion to
`claude/TERMINAL-06-spec.md` §5 (firmware status) and to every concept-plate set — MIMI-06 and
SCALER-06 inherit this catalogue, and **QUADRANT-D contributes a family of its own (§5)**.
 
Live, running versions of all of these:
**https://claude.ai/code/artifact/be830226-8f69-407f-8402-f4884e6fd099** (TERMINAL-06 plate 01 — flip
effects, glitch, sweep, colon fade, backlight breathe, ИН-15 idle cycling, all on real timings).
 
**Why this document exists.** Three separate documents were quoting effect behaviour from memory,
one of them wrongly (see the `DOT_BRIGHT` correction below). The effects are also the single
most-commented-on feature in nixie clock reviews and the whole content of listing slot 10, so what
they actually do is commercial information, not trivia.
 
**Rev 2 (05.09.26) — the QUADRANT entry in §5 was wrong and is replaced.** It was written for the
ИН-17 wristwatch, whose premise was *"total tube on-time on the watch is ~2 minutes a day, so
poisoning risk is low."* That product is withdrawn (`claude/QUADRANT-wrist-feasibility.md` Rev 3)
and QUADRANT-D is a **desk object on static drive at 100 % duty** — the highest cathode on-time of
anything in the range, not the lowest. The premise is inverted, so the effect it justified is
replaced by the pinwheel sweep, and a genuinely new family — **two-axis transitions** — is added,
because a 2 × 2 is the shop's first display that is not a row. See `claude/QUADRANT-D-spec.md` §4.
 
**Rev 3 (05.09.26) — the catalogue has been assuming a limitation that QUADRANT-D removes.**
Every effect in this file dims a **whole tube**, because on a multiplexed display that is the only
kind of dimming there is. QUADRANT-D drives forty private cathode lines from HV shift registers, so
it can set the brightness of **one cathode**, and therefore light two digits in one tube at
different levels. That is a capability, not an effect, and §0 below records it before the effects
that depend on it. See `claude/QUADRANT-D-spec.md` §4.0.
 
**Source files:** `firmware/nixieClock_2_v2.5/effects.ino`, `glitch.ino`, and the settings block of
`nixieClock_2_v2.5.ino`. Line references are to the synced GitHub copy in this project.

## 0. What kind of dimming the hardware allows — read this before the effects
 
| Drive | What can be dimmed | Products |
|---|---|---|
| **Anode multiplex** (К155ИД1 + optos) | the **whole tube** — `indiDimm` is per-tube because the anode is what's switched | TERMINAL-06, and everything inherited from AlexGyver |
| **Static, HV shift registers** | **any individual cathode**, by rewriting the register with binary code modulation | QUADRANT-D |
 
The second row is new to the shop and it is why §5 gains an effect the source has no equivalent
for. Mechanics, from the QUADRANT-D spec: 64-bit chain, **16 µs per rewrite at 4 MHz**, BCM at
8 planes per frame ≈ 800 writes/s ≈ **0 % CPU on an RP2040's PIO+DMA**.
 
**Two constraints that shape every effect built on it:**
 
1. **Usable depth is probably 4–5 bits, not 8.** A nixie needs time to ionise; at 100 Hz the LSB
   plane is ~39 µs and the glow may simply not form. Characterise the floor on the bench and treat
   it as the resolution, not as a bug.
2. **Never light two cathodes in one tube at the same instant** — they would share one anode
   resistor's ~1.4 mA and the nearer cathode may hog it. **Time-interleave them inside the frame.**
   Above ~100 Hz the eye integrates it identically and the current-sharing question never arises.

## 1. Flip effects — what happens when a digit changes
 
Selected at runtime, stored in EEPROM, cycled by a button in stock firmware and (per Rev C) by
rotary position 3 in TERMINAL-06. `FLIP_SPEED[] = {0, 130, 50, 40, 70, 70}` — milliseconds per
step of each effect's timer, indexed by effect number.
 
| # | Name (source) | Step | What actually happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | нет эффекта | — | `sendTime()` writes the digits straight out. Nothing crossfades. |
| 1 | плавное угасание и появление | **130 ms** | Only tubes whose digit *changed* ramp `indiDimm` down to 0, swap, and ramp back to `indiMaxBright`. Unchanged tubes never flicker. Source comment recommends 100–150. |
| 2 | перемотка по порядку числа | **50 ms** | Each changed tube decrements its digit — 9, 8, 7 … wrapping through 0 — until it equals the target. Tubes finish at different times, so the row settles raggedly. Recommended 50–80. |
| 3 | перемотка по порядку катодов | **40 ms** | Walks the *physical* cathode stack via `cathodeMask`, stepping the index toward the target rather than the numeral. **The only effect that makes sense exclusively on a nixie** — the glow steps through the depth of the tube instead of through arithmetic. Recommended 30–50. |
| 4 | поезд (train) | **70 ms** | Whole reading shifts one place right per tick while the leftmost tube goes dark, until the row is empty; then new digits feed in at the left, **last digit first**, sliding right to their homes. 8 ticks on 4 tubes. Recommended 50–170. |
| 5 | резинка (elastic band) | **70 ms** | One digit at a time is stretched along the row to the right edge and flicked off, rightmost source first; then each new digit is fed in at the left and stretched to its place, deepest first. **21 stages** on 4 tubes. Recommended 50–150. |
 
### Cathode masks — needed for effect 3, and easy to get wrong
 
```
BOARD_TYPE 0  digitMask   {7, 3, 6, 4, 1, 9, 8, 0, 5, 2}   cathodeMask {1, 6, 2, 7, 5, 0, 4, 9, 8, 3}
BOARD_TYPE 1  digitMask   {2, 8, 1, 9, 6, 4, 3, 5, 0, 7}   cathodeMask {1, 6, 2, 7, 5, 0, 4, 9, 8, 3}
BOARD_TYPE 2  digitMask   {9, 8, 0, 5, 4, 7, 3, 6, 2, 1}   cathodeMask {1, 0, 2, 9, 3, 8, 4, 7, 5, 6}
```
 
`cathodeMask` is the physical stacking order of the cathodes inside the tube — identical for both
ИН-12 board types, different for ИН-14. **TERMINAL-06's board is BOARD_TYPE 0**, corrected on the
bench 01.09.26; the firmware shipped as `1`, which was the cause of the scrambled digits in early
testing.
 
**ИН-17's cathode order has never been measured.** Effect 3 on the seconds tubes will step through
the wrong order until `secCathodeMap` is run — and it will look almost right, which is worse than
looking wrong. Either measure it or exclude the seconds tubes from effect 3 specifically.
**Rev 2: this now blocks two products.** QUADRANT-D is four ИН-17 and nothing else, so effect 3 —
its cheapest differentiator — is unavailable until the same one-evening measurement is taken. The
work is shared; do it once, for both.
 
### Six-tube port
 
TERMINAL-06 generalises effects 4 and 5 from 4 tubes to 6. Effects 1–3 are per-tube and generalise
for free. **Seconds are excluded from transition effects entirely** — a digit that changes every
second should step instantly, not crossfade, or the display never settles.
 
**Rev 2 caveat: effects 4 and 5 assume a row.** They are defined by "shift one place right", which
has no meaning on a 2 × 2 without deciding what "right" is. See §5.

## 2. Glitch — the thing reviewers write about
 
`glitch.ino`. Settings: `GLITCH_ALLOWED` (button-toggled), `GLITCH_MIN 30`, `GLITCH_MAX 120` seconds.
 
- Fires at a random interval **30–120 s**.
- Picks **one random tube** (`glitchIndic = random(0,4)`).
- Blinks it **2–6 times** (`glitchMax = random(2,6)`), toggling `indiDimm` between 0 and
  `indiMaxBright`.
- Interval between blinks: `random(1,6) * 20` ms → **20–100 ms**, re-randomised every blink.
- Suppressed unless `secs > 7 && secs < 55`, so it never collides with a minute flip.
That suppression window is worth keeping in any port: the glitch reads as a failing tube, and a
failing tube *during* a transition reads as a broken product.

## 3. Anti-poisoning sweep — maintenance that photographs well
 
Settings: `BURN_PERIOD 15` minutes, `BURN_LOOPS 3`, `BURN_TIME 10` ms.
 
Every 15 minutes, every tube runs 0→9 **three times** at **10 ms** a step — about **300 ms** of
controlled chaos, then straight back to the time. Also runs at power-on.
 
This is not decoration. A cathode that never fires sputters and goes cloudy, and on a product with
a ten-unit run and no returns budget that is a warranty problem, not an aesthetic one. It also
happens to be the best three seconds of the listing video, which is why it opens slot 10.

## 4. Colon and backlight
 
| Setting | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `DOT_TIME` | 500 ms | colon period |
| `DOT_TIMER` | 20 ms | brightness step — **the colon fades, it does not blink** |
| `DOT_BRIGHT` / `_N` | **35 / 15** | day / night. See correction below |
| `BACKL_BRIGHT` / `_N` | 180 / 20 | day / night |
| `BACKL_STEP` | 2 | breathing step |
| `BACKL_TIME` | 5000 ms | breathing period |
| `BACKL_PAUSE` | 600 ms | dark pause between breaths |
| `BACKL_MODE` | 0 / 1 / 2 | breathing · constant · off |
| `NIGHT_START` / `NIGHT_END` | 23 / 7 | night dimming window |
| `INDI_BRIGHT` / `_N` | 23 / 3 | digit brightness, 0–24. **At 24 you get phantom digits** |
 
**Correction, standing since 28.08.26.** `DOT_BRIGHT` / `DOT_BRIGHT_N` in the pushed source are
still **35 / 15** — the original LED-era values. The **170 / 90** figures that appear in
`claude/TERMINAL-06-spec.md` §5 and in `terminal-06.html` are an unverified *target* for a neon
colon, not a value anyone has flashed. Tune on the bench at prototype step P6 against the real
ИНС-1 pair. Do not let 170/90 propagate any further as if it were settled.
 
**Rev 2:** QUADRANT-D has no backlight at all and its night dimming is **LDR-driven and
continuous**, not a 23:00–07:00 window — so `NIGHT_START`/`NIGHT_END` and the whole `BACKL_*` block
do not port to it. Its brightness is one global PWM on the HV driver's blanking pin, which is also
why it cannot develop a tube-to-tube brightness mismatch in firmware.

## 5. Effects added by this shop, not inherited
 
### ИН-15 idle symbol cycling (TERMINAL-06 Rev D)
 
Not in the AlexGyver source — new code, not yet written. With all 18 cathodes of the ИН-15Б/ИН-15А
pair individually switchable through two MCP23017 expanders, rotary position 1 (Normal) cycles the
pair through its glyph set as an ambient animation while the clock is idle.
 
- ИН-15Б: **A, W, F, Hz, H, V, S, Ω** (8 glyphs)
- ИН-15А: **μ, −, +, %, n, m, M, K, Р, П** (10 glyphs)
**Open, and it is a product question not a code question:** if the animation runs continuously, a
glance may catch it mid-cycle on an unrelated glyph instead of the correct A or P. Two honest paths
— cycle only after N seconds of no input and snap back on activity, or accept the trade because the
real time reference is the digit tubes. Not resolved. The live page above runs the "always cycling"
version so the trade can be judged by eye rather than argued about.
 
### ~~Slot-machine glance sweep (QUADRANT)~~ — WITHDRAWN, Rev 2
 
Written for the ИН-17 wristwatch on the premise that *"total tube on-time on the watch is ~2 minutes
a day, so poisoning risk is low"*, with a short slot-machine cycle at the start of each glance
serving as both signature animation and token cathode exercise. **The wristwatch is withdrawn and
the premise is inverted:** QUADRANT-D is a desk object on **static drive at 100 % duty**, the
highest cathode on-time in the range. Nothing here survives the change. Replaced by the two entries
below.
 
### Pinwheel sweep (QUADRANT-D)
 
The §3 anti-poisoning cycle — 0→9 three times, 10 ms a step — run with a **90° phase offset per
tube**, so the glow appears to rotate around the square. Same maintenance function, same duration,
and it is the clearest single demonstration that a 2 × 2 is not a short row.
 
Two scheduling options, and they are a product decision rather than a code one:
 
- **`BURN_PERIOD 15` as inherited** — four sweeps an hour, maximum cathode coverage, which is what
  static drive needs.
- **On the hour only** — the maintenance routine reads as a chime you can see, which is a listing
  bullet, at a quarter of the coverage.
Recommendation: **run both** — hourly at full length as the visible event, and a short background
sweep on the 15-minute timer. The cost is 300 ms of display time and nothing else.
 
### Two-axis transitions (QUADRANT-D) — new family
 
Effects 4 and 5 are defined by *"shift one place right"*, which is a statement about a row. The
shop's first square display can do things no row can, and this is new inventory, not a re-skin:
 
Positions: `0` HH-tens (top-left) · `1` HH-units · `2` MM-tens · `3` MM-units.
 
| Name | Order / motion | Ticks | Needs §0 per-cathode? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dissolve** | per tube: the old digit interleaved down while the new one comes up, two numerals visible at two *depths* | ~16 @ 30 ms | **yes — and this is the one the shop has never been able to build** |
| **Pinwheel flip** | 0 → 1 → 3 → 2, 90° apart | 4 @ 60 ms | no |
| **Diagonal wipe** | (0,3) then (1,2) | 2 @ 90 ms | no |
| **Orbit** | all four digits step one place clockwise, N laps, then land | 4N + 1 | no |
| **Fold** | top row falls into the bottom row, new values rise out | ~6 @ 70 ms | no |
| **Column then row** | HH as a pair, then MM as a pair — the group break is *shown*, which is how the display gets away with no separator when the colon is dark | 2 | no |
 
**Dissolve deserves its own paragraph.** AlexGyver's effect 1 ramps `indiDimm` — the whole tube
goes dark and comes back. Dissolve fades *between digits inside one envelope*: for a moment you see
the outgoing numeral and the incoming one at once, at different depths in the glass, one giving way
to the other. **It is the most physically nixie thing in this catalogue and it is structurally
impossible on a multiplexed display**, because a multiplexed tube has exactly one brightness. It is
a bench question — see §0's two constraints — and if it survives the bench it leads the listing
video.
 
**Constraint that comes free with static drive:** none of these can band on camera at any frame
rate, because nothing is multiplexed. Effects 4 and 5's "film the train or the elastic band" rule
(§rules 5) exists because slow per-tube brightness ramps do not read at 60 fps — that still applies
to *speed*, but not to *safety*. Film any of them.
 
**Blocked on the same measurement as effect 3:** none of the above needs `cathodeMask`, but the one
effect that is exclusively nixie does, and QUADRANT-D is four ИН-17 and nothing else. Measure the
ИН-17 cathode order once and it serves both products.
 
### The ring (SCALER-06)
 
Not an animation at all, and that is the point — the decatron's glow is stepped by the events
themselves, one position per detected particle. **The only "effect" in the shop's catalogue that
cannot be faked in firmware**, and the only one whose motion carries information. Its one firmware
parameter is the divider in front of it, needed because the ring cannot step faster than the low
kilohertz.
 
**Rev 2 addition — the same is true of QUADRANT-D's DIAL module** (`claude/QUADRANT-D-spec.md`
§6.4), for a reason worth writing down: an ОГ-3's ten counting cathodes are **commoned to one pin**,
so the glow cannot be addressed, only stepped by guide pulses, and the only position readable
electrically is the index cathode. One detent of the knob is one step of the glow. **The
impossibility of faking it is the feature.**

## Rules that came out of writing this down
 
1. **Quote the constant, not the memory.** Three documents were carrying `DOT_BRIGHT 170/90` as
   fact. One `grep` settled it.
2. **An effect that walks the cathode stack needs the cathode stack measured.** Effect 3 is the most
   nixie-specific thing in the catalogue and the most likely to be silently wrong on any tube whose
   `cathodeMask` was assumed.
3. **Exclude fast-changing digits from slow effects.** Seconds never crossfade.
4. **Suppress the glitch around transitions.** A stutter during a flip reads as a fault.
5. **Effects 4 and 5 are the ones that read at 60 fps.** Effect 1 does not — a 130 ms/step
   brightness ramp is invisible against a camera shutter. Film the train or the elastic band.
6. **New, Rev 3: ask what the drive topology lets you dim before writing an effect.** This whole
   file quietly assumed whole-tube dimming for five revisions, because that is all a multiplex can
   do. Static drive dims a cathode. **An architecture change hands back capabilities as well as
   taking work away — go looking for them.**
7. **New, Rev 2: an effect inherits its form factor's premise, and the premise dies with the form
   factor.** The slot-machine sweep was correct for a watch lit two minutes a day and actively wrong
   for the same concept on a desk at 100 % duty. **When a concept moves, re-derive its animations
   from its duty cycle, not from its old entry in this file.**
8. **New, Rev 2: a row effect is not automatically a square effect.** "Shift one place right" has no
   definition on a 2 × 2. Ask what the display's shape allows before porting anything.
