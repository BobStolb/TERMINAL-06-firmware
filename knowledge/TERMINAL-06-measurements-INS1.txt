# TERMINAL-06 — measurement capture: ИНС-1
 
Third entry in the Section 3 capture-sheet series (`TERMINAL-06-cad-component-library.md`),
same format as the IN-12 and IN-17 sheets.
 
Sample: 1 of your pulled ИНС-1 tubes, Vogel digital caliper, 0.01 mm resolution.
 
## Worth flagging before the table
 
This isn't a symmetric wire-ended part like ИНС-1 was assumed to be in the original
capture sheet. One end is a fine flexible lead; the other terminates in a **metal
ferrule/cap**, with a rounded glass dome — the actual glow surface — sitting past the cap.
That's a different construction from "two bare leads," closer to a subminiature lamp base
on one side. Worth confirming before modelling starts:
 
- **Which end is the real mounting/electrical reference?** If the cap is what gets
  soldered or clipped into the holder, the part's origin should follow the cap, not a
  jig-defined lead plane the way ИНС-1 is currently grouped with IN-17 in the library
  guide's origin rules.
- **How is the cap retained** — crimped, soldered, cemented to the glass? Affects whether
  it's safe to treat as rigid/intrinsic to the tube (it almost certainly is) versus
  something that could shift like IN-17's lead spacer turned out to be.
