# TS06 pair case, concept A

A parametric case for the through-hole pair, TS06-DISP riding on TS06-DRV. It is drawn around
the boards as they are in this repository. The brief is the case section of
`Claude outputs/TS06-pair-review.md`. It keeps Rev F's idea: two cheeks carry everything, and
nothing else is structure. The module (both boards) hangs from four bosses and lifts out
backwards as one piece. A brow, a top plate, a trench frame, a base and a rear panel close the
box between the cheeks.

![front](out/iso.png)

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `case_pair.py` | The generator. It reads the boards, holds every dimension with its source, derives the geometry, runs the checks, and writes `params.scad`, `checks.md` and the drawings. |
| `case.scad` | The OpenSCAD model. It includes `params.scad` and derives the same numbers. It echoes them so they can be compared with the generator's (`--render` does this). |
| `params.scad` | Generated. Every dimension is a named variable with its kind and source, plus the board data in world coordinates. |
| `boards.json` | Generated by `--extract`. Holes, parts and courtyards of TS06-DISP, TS06-DRV and TS06-FASCIA in world coordinates, with hashes of the files they came from. |
| `checks.md` | Generated. The envelope, every interference check, every screw, and every dimension with its source. |
| `out/front.svg`, `section.svg`, `plan.svg`, `exploded.svg` | Dimensioned drawings at 1:1: front elevation, side section through M10 with the ±10° sight lines and every cheek screw, plan section through the jack axis, exploded side view with the screws per part. |
| `out/*.png` | OpenSCAD views: `iso`, `iso_rear`, `front`, `exploded`, `module`. |
| `out/*.stl` | Printable parts in world position: `cheek_l`, `cheek_r`, `brow`, `top`, `trench`, `base`, `rear`, and `fascia_blank` (the PETG fit proxy that cad-component-library §5 asks for). `fascia_frame` is variant D's (below). |

**Frame.** X and Y are the FreeCAD assembly's frame, the one the review uses. X runs across the
front from the clock's left, where TS06-DISP's x = 0. Y points up: TS06-DISP's top edge is at
78 and TS06-DRV's bottom edge is at 4. Z is depth: 0 at the ИН-12 glass front, positive toward
the back. OpenSCAD maps x = X, y = Z, z = Y, so the clock faces -y.

**Where the board numbers come from.** `tools/mkpcb_disp.py` and `tools/mkpcb_drv.py` are
imported as modules. That runs their placement and hand-laid copper, with no router and no file
written; the same generators write the committed `PCB/TS06-DISP` and `PCB/TS06-DRV` boards.
`TS06-FASCIA.kicad_pcb` is parsed directly, and `FASCIA_PCB=<board>` puts another fascia in its
place. Parts are keyed by reference; each TS06-DRV part also carries whether any of its pads is on
a 185 V net (the generator's own net classes) and the height its footprint's descr states. A
footprint the height table does not know takes that height, so a new L1 footprint is picked up,
not refused.

## Review suggestions implemented

1. **Cheeks and boards.** Two 6 mm cheeks. TS06-DRV's H6 and H8 go to the left cheek (world X 3.5;
   Y 7.5 and 81.5), and H5 and H7 to the right (X 187.9; Y 7.5 and 100.5). Each lands on an 8 mm
   boss with an M3 heat-set insert, in front of TS06-DRV. The display rides on its four 11 mm
   standoffs, so the pair lifts out as one module.
2. **The rear panel is a board.** It is a 1.6 mm FR4 blank. Seven 2.0 mm vent slots sit over the
   logic side (X 6.9–35.9, Y 85–100, over the Nano), and the panel carries the 12 V / 185 V /
   "unplug, wait 15 s" label. It is fixed along its long edges (below).
3. **Connector openings.**
   - DC jack: a Ø9 hole through, and a Ø14 counterbore **from the outside** that leaves a 1.5 mm
     web. The plug engages 7.2 mm. The review's "pocket from inside" would leave the outer face
     where it is, and the plug would engage only 2.7 mm.
   - USB: a 12 x 9 slot, **open to the cheek's rear edge**. The mini-B receptacle sits 1.9 mm
     inside the cheek, so a closed window would trap the module.
4. **Brow and trench.**
   - The soffit is 0.8 mm above the tall glass. Behind the ИН-17 pair a valance rib comes down to
     Y 74.0, the "top 4 mm" of the review, and hides XP12. Over the ИН-12s and ИН-15s the rib
     cannot be there: those tubes would not come out past it.
   - The left trench wall is at X 3.0 and hides XP11. The right wall is at X 182.035: ИН-15А's
     glass plus the +0.4 allowance plus 0.5. It hides the H4 screw and XP12's last pins (X 183.55).
   - A sill at Y 39.0 hides XP21–25. That is the review's "≥ 38", kept under the ИН-17 LEDs.
   - The brow face is safety orange (spec §6); the top plate behind it is a separate part, black
     like the chassis.
5. **Fascia.**
   - The fascia is tied to the cheeks with four M2.5 bosses at its real holes. It is centred under
     the tube row (`FASCIA_X0` 4.305, X 4.3–180.3): on the middle of H10 and ИН-15А, X 92.3, not
     on the boards' middle, 95.7. TS06-DISP's margins are 3.5 mm left of the glass and 10.3 right.
   - The fascia lead runs from DRV J1 (top entry, X 44–54, Y 9) down to the floor, diagonally
     across it, and up into the fascia's J1 at X 156.3.
6. **Heat.** Top-of-panel vents only, as the review says; over the logic, not over the 185 V
   converter.
7. **Service.** Take the rear panel off (6 screws) and the module screws out (4). Draw the module
   back, unplug DRV J1 from below, and lift it out.

## Case review (30.09.26): what changed

- **F7, fixings.** The cheek's M3 holes used to land on the edges of 3 mm plates (the top bar at
  Y 108.5, the base at −8.8), and the M3 x 8 into the 3.5 mm left trench wall reached X 3.5,
  0.025 mm from H10's glass (3.475). Every cheek screw now goes into a heat-set insert in an end
  block of at least 8 mm on the part it holds:
  - base: 8 x 11 x 8 blocks on the floor at each cheek, front (Z 10) and rear (Z 59.2);
  - trench: 8 x 8 blocks under H10's and ИН-15А's glass, through the sill (Y 36.7–44.7, 1.0 below
    the glass), where the wall is too thin for an insert;
  - brow: 8 x 8 blocks behind the face at Y 93.5;
  - top plate: 8 x 8 blocks at the front (Z 20) and the rear (Z 59.2), and a full-width 8 mm lip
    at the rear. That lip sits above TS06-DRV's top edge, which is why the case grew 2.5 mm.

  The trench screw now stays 3.5 mm from H10's glass; check 10 measures every screw's whole shank
  against the glass and the boards, and none comes within 2.5 mm. Screw lengths are on
  `section.svg` and `exploded.svg`, and in the table below.
- **F8, lead-ins.** 45° x 3 mm lead-ins on the rear edges the module passes within 1 mm: the
  soffit's (0.8 over the tall glass; the soffit ends in a 2 mm lip that carries it), the left trench
  wall's inner edge (0.475 from H10), and the sill's, only at X 105.7–111.5 and 126.2–132.0 where
  HL5 and HL6's flanges pass 0.59 over it. A full-width sill lead-in would let XP21–25's joints be
  seen from about 20° to 52° above; these stay 2.65 mm clear of the pads. The new trench blocks'
  rear top edges have one too. What they cost in sight lines: XP11's joints now show from the
  right past 7.8° (was 28.8°), only through H10's glass; TS06-DRV's front face at Y 95 shows from
  48–52° below, through the soffit's lead-in.
- **F9, printed spacing, FR4 holes.** The printed crossmembers set the 192.4 mm between the
  cheeks; at 0.4% shrink they come out 0.77 mm short, and TS06-DRV's H5–H8 float only 0.1. So:
  **screw the module in first, so the DRV locates the cheeks**, then tighten the crossmember screws;
  scale the crossmembers (brow, top plate, trench, base, frame) by +0.4% in X in the slicer, or by
  whatever a 150 mm test bar in the same filament says. The FR4 rear panel's holes are slotted
  ±0.6 along X. The crossmembers themselves are held by screws along X through the cheeks, which a
  slot cannot float; the module-first order and the scale do that job.
- **m1, rotary.** `ROTARY_D` 25.00, from `knowledge/TERMINAL-06-spec.txt` §6, MEASURE BEFORE
  ORDERING PANELS, which supersedes the withdrawn 26.94. `ROTARY_DEPTH` 22: the lugs sit at least
  11.3 behind the fascia and are 10 long. The sill notch is re-derived: it steps back to Z 3.55 (was
  8.21), over X 20.8–47.8, leaving a 2.5 mm slot behind the fascia's top edge (was 7.2).
- **m4, rear panel.** Six M2.5 x 6: three along the bottom into the base's rear lip and three
  along the top into the top plate's, at X 3.5, 95.7 and 187.9. Its longest free edge is 92.2 mm
  (was 198.4). None go into the cheeks' 6 mm rear edges: an M2.5 insert (Ø3.5) there has 1.25 mm
  of wall. In the lips it has 2.25. The vents moved from X 100–130, over VT21, C7, VD1 and R62, to
  X 6.9–35.9 over the Nano; no 185 V part is within 5 mm of them.
- **m5, top plate.** A separate part, black; the brow face stays safety orange.
- **n1.** `LEAD_LEN` 190 (the BOM's 180–200): 51 mm of slack, was 11. Every output regenerated.
  Stale text fixed: the generator's docstring (TS06-DRV is not "the older 100 x 70 driver half"),
  the front elevation's fascia registration note and label, the plan's fascia outline, the BOM
  line on case screws (it now lists all four, H5–H8).

## Variant D: the fascia frame (`FASCIA_FRAME=1`, off by default)

With it on, a printed frame sits between the cheeks, raked 12°. Its pocket continues the trench
walls (X 3.0–182.035) and takes a 179 x 40 panel in a rabbet: a 2 mm ledge behind its sides and

## Regenerate

```
python3 3d/case-pair/case_pair.py --extract   # re-read the boards (imports tools/, writes boards.json only)
python3 3d/case-pair/case_pair.py             # params.scad, checks.md, out/*.svg
python3 3d/case-pair/case_pair.py --render    # + out/*.stl and out/*.png (OpenSCAD 2021.01, xvfb-run)
python3 3d/case-pair/case_pair.py IN12_SEAT=0 # what-if: prints the envelope, writes nothing
openscad -D 'PART="cheek_r"' -o cheek_r.stl 3d/case-pair/case.scad
openscad -D FASCIA_FRAME=1 -D 'PART="cheek_l"' -o cheek_l.stl 3d/case-pair/case.scad   # variant D's cheeks
```

The generator needs only Python 3's standard library. OpenSCAD comes from apt
(`apt-get install openscad`). The PNG views need an X server, so `--render` wraps them in
`xvfb-run`. The `--render` step also checks that `case.scad` derives the same numbers as the
generator, and prints `ok` or `MISMATCH` for each. Variant D is scored in `checks.md` section 12
on every run, whatever `FASCIA_FRAME` is set to.
